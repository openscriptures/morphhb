Miqra according to the Masorah (MAM)
	2026-11-03

MAPM.xml is the text of the Hebrew Bible according to Miqra according to the Masorah,
prepared by Seth (Avi) Kadish. MAM-basics, https://github.com/bdenckla/MAM-basics,
generated it on 2026-11-03, at commit 83caf1689a7c8e382254af07ecb5373819ce3f07, from MAM's text on Hebrew Wikisource,
as MAM-basics then had it. There it is MAM-morphhb/MAPM.xml, which is regenerated by hand,
so a copy there may be newer than this one.

This file keeps the OSIS form of David Troidl's conversion of 2014-02-19, which it replaces.
That form preserves the Hebrew Bible's structure in three parts: the Torah, the Prophets and
the Writings. The Torah is divided into sections for the weekly Torah readings, and Psalms
into its five books. Samuel, Kings, the Twelve, Ezra-Nehemiah and Chronicles are groups of
books. The Hebrew titles are in title elements, and the Hebrew chapter and verse numbers in
the n attribute of the chapter and verse start milestones. Psalms, Proverbs and Job 3:2-42:6
are laid out as poetic lines.

What differs from the 2014 file:
- The verse numbering is BHS's throughout, as in WLC. Eight English notes of type "variant"
  say where MAM's own numbering differs. Joshua 21:36-37, which MAM's text does not have,
  are left out.
- Each qere is marked, <seg type="x-qere">, beside its <seg type="x-ketiv">, in MAM's order.
- The songs are ordinary verse lines, not tables.
- The good endings of Isaiah, Malachi, Ecclesiastes and Lamentations are segs of type
  "x-good-ending" inside each book's last verse.
- Notes mark scroll differences, words with large, small or hung letters, and silluq
  before meteg.
